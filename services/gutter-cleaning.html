Services  /  Gutter Cleaning

Gutter Cleaning in Grey-Bruce, Ontario

Clear, working gutters that protect the house — top to bottom.

Get a free quote (519) 706-3325Call or text

Done properly, not just scooped.

We take the guards off where they're on, clear every length by hand, and bag the debris instead of dropping it on your beds. Then we flush the runs and downspouts so we know the water is actually leaving the house, not just sitting somewhere new.

Everything goes back the way we found it, and we tell you straight if we spot a loose hanger, a leak, or a length that needs a repair.

Blocked gutters are how small problems become expensive ones: overflow down the siding, water in the basement, ice in the eaves. We clear yours out properly, flush them through, and check that every downspout actually runs.

What's included
  • Guards removed and reset where fitted
  • All gutters cleared by hand and bagged
  • Downspouts checked and flushed clear
  • A flush-through to confirm proper flow
  • A heads-up on any leaks or loose sections
  • Beds and walks left clean — debris taken with us
