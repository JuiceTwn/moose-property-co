Seasonal Care

Spring and fall cleanups, plus upkeep on a schedule that suits you.

Get a free quote (519) 706-3325Call or text

One crew, all season.

Spring is the reset: beds cleaned out, winter debris cleared, the first cut and edge of the year. Fall is leaves, gutters and putting the property to bed for winter so spring is easier.

In between, we keep it on a schedule that suits you — one point of contact, one standard, the whole property handled so you never have to line up three different crews.

The property never really stops asking for something. Seasonal care is us getting ahead of it — the big spring and fall cleanups that reset the place, plus the ongoing upkeep that keeps it there.

What's included
  • Spring cleanups — beds, debris, first cut and edge
  • Fall cleanups — leaves cleared and hauled
  • Gutters cleared heading into winter
  • Beds, borders and shrubs kept tidy through the season
  • One schedule covering the whole property
  • One point of contact for all of it
